Two Plates

A calorie tracker for two, made for the phone

I built Two Plates for my girlfriend and me. We both want to lose fat and build muscle, and we use it every day on our iPhones to log meals, read our InBody results and keep each other on track. The phone here runs the real app with sample data. Tap around.

  • Log food in secondsSnap a photo or describe the meal and AI estimates calories and protein. It knows Taiwanese food. Barcodes work too.
  • Read InBody results from a photoFat and muscle trends, and a weekly review that links the change to food and sleep.
  • A coach that asks firstChat about your numbers. It proposes changes as cards, and nothing changes until you tap Apply.
  • Sleep and two phones in syncSleep comes in from Apple Health through iPhone Shortcuts, and both phones share the same data.
